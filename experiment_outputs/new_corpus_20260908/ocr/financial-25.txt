G. Competitive Bidding

To ensure that IEEE receives the best value, a minimum of three written bids should be
obtained for all purchases valued at or above USD50,000 in total. For purchases totaling
USD250,000 or more, a formal competitive bidding process is required, unless a
documented compelling business justification for proceeding without competition is
provided and approved by the Management Council Representative of the relevant
Organizational Unit. Examples of compelling business justifications include situations
where a supplier is the only provider of a specific product or service (sole source), or
where the costs associated with changing suppliers would be prohibitively high.

Contracts for conference hotels, catering, and convention centers are exempt from this
requirement.

The Procurement Operations Department, along with authorized staff and volunteer
subject matter experts will jointly manage and document the formal bidding process.
Supplier selection will be based on various criteria, including but not limited to
professional qualifications, experience, reliability, prior performance, and price. The
Organizational Unit Management Council Representative must approve the selected
supplier. All correspondence related to this process shall be electronically filed in the CMS
for central record retention.
