INTRODUCTION

IEEE must always demonstrate prudence, care, transparency, and responsibility with the
funds with which it is entrusted. IEEE has a fiduciary responsibility not only to its members
and the technical communities that it serves, but as a 501(c)(3) public charity, it has a
responsibility to the public to deliver on its mission today and well into the future.

The following procedures affecting the financial matters of the IEEE have been incorporated
into this IEEE Finance Operations Manual (FOM). Procedures related to investment matters
are included in the IEEE Investment Operations Manual (IOM). These procedures shall be
developed, updated, and approved by the various responsible Committees of IEEE, with
oversight by the IEEE Finance Committee (IEEE FinCom). In accordance with IEEE Policies
related to the financial operations of the IEEE, the members of the IEEE Board of Directors
shall be informed of changes to these procedures thirty (30) days prior to implementation by
the IEEE Finance Committee (IEEE Bylaws I-300.4.6).

Operational policies and procedures of the respective organizational units (OUs) shall not
conflict with these procedures.

It is expected that volunteers and staff involved in operational planning understand the
resulting financial implications and familiarize themselves with these procedures in their
entirety, and with the corresponding policies found in Section 11 of the IEEE Policies. For
ease of use of this manual, those policies have been inserted in the appropriate
corresponding section in this document in Bold/Italic font.

Copies of these procedures shall be available on IEEE’s website. Note: All dollar amounts in
this manual refer to US dollars. (USD = US dollars)
