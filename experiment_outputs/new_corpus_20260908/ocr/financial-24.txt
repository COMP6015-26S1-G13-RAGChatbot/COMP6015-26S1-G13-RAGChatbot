E. Other Required Approvals
1) IEEE Suppliers

a) Prior to providing any goods or services to IEEE, suppliers shall be registered
and approved by means of the Supplier Management System.

b) This process is managed by the Procurement Operations Department and is
supported and administered by staff. The Supplier Management System shall be
the primary secure portal and repository for the collection, maintenance, and
retention of supplier tax and other sensitive business-related information.
Further clarification is documented on the Procurement Operations website
section of the IEEE intranet.

2) Technology Related

a) Contracts that involve the purchase of Technology Related Goods and Services
(as defined in FOM 9, Section A) require approval by the IT Tech
Standardization and Management team. For questions please contact:
ittsm@ieee.org.

b) Contracts that involve PII require approval by IT Security.
3) Independent Contractors

a) Any individual, sole proprietor, or single owner LLC, shall be reviewed and
approved as an independent contractor by the IEEE Legal & Compliance
Department prior to the start of services and contract execution.

b) This process is managed by the Legal & Compliance Department. Further
clarification is documented on the Legal & Compliance website section of the
IEEE intranet.

4) Purchase Orders

a) As part of internal controls for all purchases of goods and services with an
aggregate purchase value of USD1,000 and over require an authorized
purchase order (PO) before work begins or goods are shipped. IEEE OUs/staff
must not commence services or process orders until they receive a formal PO.
IEEE OUs eligible to participate in the Concentration Banking program, as
defined in FOM 3.A.2, are generally exempt from the PO requirement.

b) This process is managed by the Procurement Operations Department and
supported and administered by staff. Further clarification is documented on the
Procurement Operations website section of the IEEE intranet.
