5. General Process

The IEEE Executive Director and Professional staff will start the budget process by
using the latest available forecast of the current year. This process should include a
connection between the activity and the impact on revenue and expenses.

During the budget development process, there will be regular communication with
IEEE FinCom. IEEE FinCom will oversee revisions until the budget is approved by the
IEEE Board of Directors. Periodic updates will be presented to the IEEE Board of
Directors, as appropriate. The final budget shall be submitted to the IEEE Board of
Directors for approval prior to its last meeting of the year.

The annual operating and capital budgets with supporting documentation will be
provided to the IEEE Board of Directors in preparation of their review and approval of
the budget.

6. Budget Principles
General:

a) The IEEE Operations consolidated budget will reflect the strategy of the Board of
Directors after careful consideration of short and long term impact on liquidity.

b) Non-operating activity shall include a reasonable budget for dividend and interest
income but not include any estimate for realized or unrealized gains or losses on
investments. Realized and unrealized investment gains and losses income are not
in the IEEE Operations Budget.

¢) Other non-operating expenses may include insurance and employer-made
retirement plan contributions which are outside the operating plan of IEEE.

d) Taking all of the above into account will result in the “surplus/ (deficit) before
tax”,

e) The final results of IEEE’s anticipated financial performance should be the budgeted
“net surplus after tax”.

f) IEEE Bylaw I-108.5 prescribes funding for Regions, Sections, Chapters, Affinity
Groups and Student Branches.

g) IEEE Member Dues and the IEEE-USA Assessment

Each year, the IEEE Finance Committee, as part of the budgeting process, shall
consider whether to propose a new base for the dues algorithm specified in the
Bylaws. A dues change item may be prepared by the Finance Committee and
submitted to the Board of Directors as a Bylaw revision for consideration at the
June meeting. The Finance Committee shall notify the Board of Directors whether it
intends to propose a re-basing of the algorithm at least 30 days prior to the June
Board of Directors meeting.
